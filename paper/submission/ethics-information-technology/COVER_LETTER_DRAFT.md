Please consider the manuscript **“From Conversation to Persistent Research State: Human–AI Research Collaboration, Human Responsibility, and Auditable Authorship in the AI Era”** for consideration as an Original Research Paper in *Ethics and Information Technology*.

The manuscript develops a governed Project Memory Architecture for long-running human–AI inquiry. Its central problem is how authoritative project state—including evidence, human decision semantics, current work state, authorization, and publication boundaries—can remain inspectable and recoverable when AI agents or models are replaced. The paper distinguishes project memory from agent memory, situates the proposal in prior project-memory, design-rationale, decision-provenance, and agent-memory research, and proposes agent/model substitution as a continuity stress test. It also specifies a future empirical evaluation agenda while making no claim that the architecture has already been empirically validated.

We believe the manuscript is relevant to the journal because it treats AI-assisted inquiry as an information-technology governance problem involving accountability, provenance, human decision authority, and responsible use of AI systems rather than merely as a productivity workflow.
