AI-Assisted Human Inquiry and Creation Protocol

Human-led inquiry, assisted by AI.

AHICP is a repository-grounded protocol for sustained inquiry, research, reasoning, writing, and creation with AI assistance.

人主导，AI 协助

AHICP 是一套面向长期探究、研究、推理、写作与创作的协议。人的目的、判断、批准与责任保持在中心；AI 的工作应当成为可检查、可追溯、可交接的提案与协助。

What AHICP makes durable

  • Human intention and substantive commitments
  • Evidence, decisions, approvals, and working memory
  • Zero-context onboarding for a new capable agent
  • Reusable research-project and form-profile templates
