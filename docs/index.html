AHICP
AI-Assisted Human Inquiry and Creation Protocol

让人主导探究，让 AI 成为可检查的协助。

AHICP 是一套面向长期探究、研究、推理、写作与创作的协议。它把人的目的、判断、批准与责任放在中心，并要求 AI 的工作能够被理解、核验、追溯、交接和重建。

AHICP 解决什么问题

长期的人机协作很容易变成一次性的对话：重要决定散落在聊天记录里，证据与推断混在一起，新的协作者无法知道项目为何这样推进，AI 也难以在缺少上下文时可靠接手。AHICP 将这些关键状态组织成一个以仓库为基础、以证据为依据、以人类授权为边界的工作系统。

人的意图持续存在

项目目标、范围、价值判断和不可妥协的承诺由人明确保存，而不是由模型临时猜测。

证据与决定可追溯

来源、分析、决策、批准和变更记录彼此区分，使读者能够回到依据本身。

交接不依赖记忆

新的合作者或新的 AI 能够从仓库中的说明、工作记忆和当前状态开始，而不是依赖私人对话历史。

协助不替代责任

AI 可以提出、整理、比较和执行已获授权的步骤，但人的判断、批准和责任边界始终清晰。

核心工作方式

1. 先定义问题

明确研究或创作对象、目标、范围、输出形式、时间边界与成功标准。

2. 保留来源

把原始材料、引用、数据、假设和外部链接作为可检查的项目材料保存。

3. 区分状态

区分事实、解释、假设、提案、已批准决定与尚未解决的问题。

4. 记录批准

对会改变方向、公开发布、影响他人或产生不可逆后果的行动保留明确授权。

5. 形成可交接结果

让下一位人类或 AI 知道当前状态、下一步、风险、待确认事项以及不能擅自改变的内容。

适合谁使用

AHICP 可以服务于个人研究、学术写作、长期学习、知识管理、复杂创作、教育项目以及需要持续上下文的人机协作。它不要求你使用某一家模型，也不要求把全部材料交给任何平台；你可以把它作为自己的仓库规范、项目模板和协作协议。

AI-Assisted Human Inquiry and Creation Protocol

Keep inquiry human-led, and make AI assistance inspectable.

AHICP is a repository-grounded protocol for sustained inquiry, research, reasoning, writing, and creation with AI assistance. It keeps human purpose, judgment, approval, and responsibility at the center while making AI work understandable, reviewable, traceable, transferable, and rebuildable.

What AHICP is for

Long-running human–AI work can collapse into one-off conversations. Important decisions disappear into chat history, evidence becomes mixed with interpretation, new collaborators cannot tell why a project took its current direction, and an AI system cannot safely continue without private context. AHICP turns these concerns into a repository-based working system bounded by human authorization and grounded in evidence.

Human intention endures

Goals, scope, values, and non-negotiable commitments are preserved explicitly instead of being guessed by a model at runtime.

Evidence and decisions are traceable

Sources, analysis, decisions, approvals, and changes remain distinguishable so readers can return to the basis of a claim.

Handoffs do not depend on memory

A new collaborator or capable AI can begin from the repository’s instructions, working memory, and current state rather than a private conversation.

Assistance does not replace responsibility

AI may propose, organize, compare, and execute authorized steps, but human judgment, approval, and responsibility boundaries remain explicit.

How the protocol works

1. Define the inquiry

State the object, purpose, scope, output, time boundary, and success conditions of the research or creative project.

2. Preserve sources

Keep source material, citations, data, assumptions, and external references as inspectable project material.

3. Separate states

Distinguish facts, interpretations, hypotheses, proposals, approved decisions, and unresolved questions.

4. Record approvals

Keep explicit authorization for actions that change direction, publish publicly, affect others, or create irreversible consequences.

5. Produce a handoff-ready state

Make the current state, next steps, risks, open questions, and protected commitments clear to the next human or AI collaborator.

Who can use it

AHICP can support personal research, academic writing, long-term learning, knowledge management, complex creative work, educational projects, and any human–AI collaboration that needs durable context. It does not require a particular model provider or platform, and it does not require you to surrender all material to a service. You can use it as a repository convention, project template, and collaboration protocol.
