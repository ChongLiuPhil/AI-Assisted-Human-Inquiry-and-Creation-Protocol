## OPEN

### CLR-001 — Central responsibility concept in the methodology article

**Status:** `OPEN`  
**Severity:** `BLOCKING`  
**Category:** `CONCEPT / TERMINOLOGY / TRANSLATION`

**Trigger:** methodology-article D1; the human requirement to examine human cognitive/epistemic responsibility and capability boundaries in AI-era research.

**Uncertain point:** how the article should distinguish and name work AI may perform from responsibility humans must retain.

**Candidate terminology:**

1. `cognitive labor` = **认知劳动**, for delegable search, organization, drafting, checking, formalization, etc.;
2. `epistemic responsibility` = **认识责任**, for human acceptance, judgment, authorization, and responsibility for public claims;
3. `cognitive responsibility` = **认知责任**, potentially as a broader umbrella term;
4. another terminology system supplied by the human.

**Why it matters:** affects the central question, T3, title/abstract, responsibility theory, and bilingual terminology.

**AI recommendation:** `AI-PROPOSED` — use a layered distinction between `cognitive labor / 认知劳动` and `epistemic responsibility / 认识责任`, with `cognitive responsibility / 认知责任` only as a broader descriptor when needed.

**Question for the human:** accept this layered terminology, or revise it?

**Human resolution:** `PENDING`
